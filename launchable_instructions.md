# SC25-launchables-setup
A coupon code will be provided during the workshop. Note that it may be redeemed only once per account. 

**Step 1:** Go to [http://brev.nvidia.com](http://brev.nvidia.com) and input your email to create an account.

**Step 2:** Create a new Brev Org after setting up your account. Click on the “Building Icon” in the top right corner, then click "Create a new organization." Provide a name for your Brev Org.

**Step 3:** Once you have created and named your Brev organization, go to the "Billing" tab.

**Step 4:** Scroll down and click "Redeem Code." Enter the code you were provided in the "Enter Code" field and click "Redeem." Please ensure coupon code is all lower case.
